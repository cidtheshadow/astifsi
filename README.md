# National Food Conference 2026 (NFC 2026)

> **Innovate Today. Nourish Tomorrow**  
> On the occasion of **World Food Day 2026** (15–16 October 2026)  
> Organized by **AFST(I) Longowal Chapter & Department of Food Engineering & Technology**, Sant Longowal Institute of Engineering & Technology (SLIET), Longowal, Punjab (in association with AFST(I), Mysuru).

---

## 🌾 Overview

The **National Food Conference 2026** (Hybrid Mode) brings together food scientists, technologists, engineers, academicians, researchers, industry professionals, entrepreneurs, policymakers, and students to deliberate on emerging challenges and opportunities across the food value chain.

---

## 🚀 Key Features of this Web Application

- **Editorial Aesthetic Layout**: Built with React 18, Vite, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS v4.
- **Live Countdown Timers**: Countdown to Abstract Submission (Sept 30, 2026) and Conference Opening (Oct 15, 2026).
- **10 Focus Areas Scope Grid**: Complete track breakdown with modal dialogs & domain filtering.
- **Abstract Submission Portal**: Interactive word checker & 300-word limit validator with Times New Roman 12pt specifications.
- **Venue & Travel Connectivity Matrix**: SLIET Longowal 451-acre campus spotlight with distance matrices for roadways, railways (Sangrur, Barnala, Dhuri), and airports (Chandigarh & Halwara Ludhiana).
- **Organizing & Advisory Committees**: Complete directory of leadership, patron, chair, secretary, and national advisory members.
